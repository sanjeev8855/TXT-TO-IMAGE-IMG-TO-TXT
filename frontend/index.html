Visual AI Studio

Explainable AI Forensic Verification & RAG-Guided Multimodal Synthesis

This system provides a reproducible research workbench uniting two bidirectional multimodal capabilities: Visual Understanding (Image-to-Text via VLM) and Grounded Latent Synthesis (RAG-Enhanced Text-to-Image via Diffusion), strictly coupled with an independent Explainable AI (XAI) Forensic Detection Module.

01 / Visual Understanding

Multi-Model Vision & Pixel Analysis

Extracts structured visual descriptors (subjects, lighting, composition, pixel color clustering) using Gemini Vision, Llama 3.2 90B Vision, and Kimi K3 fallback.

02 / RAG & Latent Synthesis

Optical Knowledge & Diffusion

Retrieves optical and aesthetic parameters from an indexed corpus. Sanitizes prompts against injection before rendering via iterative latent diffusion.

03 / Independent Forensics

Decoupled Patch-Level Detection

Evaluates 64×64 overlapping patches using multi-stream spatial noise residuals and 2D Fourier (FFT) grid analysis into pseudo-thermal heatmaps.

Research Protocol & Forensic Interpretation:

Forensic anomaly scores quantify statistical artifact discrepancies and model confidence. They do not claim mathematical certainty or absolute legal proof of synthetic or authentic origin. The pseudo-thermal heatmaps localize statistical artifact divergence for human-in-the-loop explainability.

Terms of Use

1. Prototype Scope: This application is provided for demonstration of multimodal machine learning and computer-vision forensics.

2. No Legal or Forensic Certifications: Results produced by the forensic anomaly detector, likelihood scores, and heatmap visualizations represent statistical inferences. They do not constitute certified legal testimony or conclusive proof of image authenticity.

3. User Content & Uploads: Users retain ownership of images uploaded to the laboratory. EXIF metadata is automatically stripped upon ingest to protect privacy. Local server storage is maintained temporarily for the duration of experiment sessions.

4. Responsible AI Usage: Users agree not to utilize this prototype to construct malicious prompts or attempt bypasses of safety boundary layers.

Privacy Policy

DRAFT FOR REVIEW

1. Image Data Handling: Uploaded images are sanitized locally; embedded EXIF location, device serial, and timestamp tags are removed before model ingestion.
